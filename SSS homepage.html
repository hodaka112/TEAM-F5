GET TO KNOW US

About Us

Sun Son Solar is a solar company based in Pasig City, founded by Katherine “Kat” Sinagaraw and her father, Santino “Sonny” Sinagaraw. We supply solar panels, inverters, batteries, racking and mounting systems, and wires. We also provide end-to-end services, including consultation, design, permitting, installation, maintenance, repair, and monitoring. Our goal is to make going solar simple, from your first inquiry to long-term support.
